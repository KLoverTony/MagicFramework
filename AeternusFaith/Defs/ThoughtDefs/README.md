# Thought Notes

Thoughts should support sacred-space, death, memory, and spirit storytelling.

Possible thought sources:

- Proper funeral or missed funeral.
- Grave tended or neglected.
- Ossuary maintained.
- Sacred room desecrated.
- Spirit manifestation witnessed.
- Family ghost protection.
- Bonewright ritual success or fallout.
- Corrupted shrine exposure.

Prefer small, flavorful mood touches over large predictable buffs.
