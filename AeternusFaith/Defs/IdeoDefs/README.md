# IdeoDefs Notes

## Faith of Aeternus

Broad, tolerant pantheon faith.

Flavor line:

> The gods are many voices in one harmony; no shrine stands alone, and no blessing is owned.

Shared values:

- Respect for burial and remembrance.
- Reverence for sacred spaces.
- Acceptance of multiple patrons within the same faith.
- Magic treated as sacred or culturally accepted.
- Corrupted Six worship treated as hostile or dangerous.
- Bonewright practices accepted only when ritually sanctioned.

Core roles:

- Hierophant: primary priest and divine conduit.
- Temple Warden: protector, assistant, shrine keeper, or ritual guardian.
- Bonewright or Cryptwarden: death specialist tied to funerary, ossuary, and undead systems.

## Corrupted Six

Hostile or tempting rival ideology.

Gameplay identity:

- Faster rewards than the pantheon.
- More visible buffs.
- Hidden or escalating corruption consequences.
- Tainted shrines, corrupted undead, void events, and spiritual contamination.
